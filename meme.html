Meme Mashup

For this meme, I used this meme of a wojak looking at their computer as the base and replaced the image of the computer at the bottom with the 67 kid. Then I used the Sackboy, a character from LittleBigPlanet, reaction as my reaction and used text with impact font to describe my reaction. I created this meme to show my usual reaction to whenever I find a new or slightly old meme. Sometimes I feel out of touch with new trends and memes, so I made this mashup to capture how I would feel at that moment. Also, I thought that the combination of this memes was amusing to me, I decided to put it together for this project.

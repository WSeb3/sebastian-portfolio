This is my logo design. I used Adobe Illustrator to create these designs. For this logo design, I decided to keep incorporate two things that I liked. That would be robots and drawing. I combined those two and tried to make the design simple. I thought a simple box and some detail would work as a robot and I had him holding a pen to represent drawing. Since this is a logo that is supposed to represent me, I thought it would make the sense for me to use the username I have used since I was in middle school.

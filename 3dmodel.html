3D Model

This is my 3D model of my old desk and room scene from memory. One thing I felt like I could have done way better was the lighting. I tried having the light come from the sliding doors and ceiling light from off screen. Since I don't know how to create a window with light coming through it, the light from the sliding door doesn't look good. Since I wanted the scene to resemble my old room, I tried creating a floor pattern and a sliding door in the wall.

Virtual City

For my virtual space, I wanted to create a space based on the small workspace for building model kits that I have on my desk. Some of the objects I chose are tools I would have used when building a model kit. The other objects are things that I like building, some memes I like, and anything that I thought would fit the scene. I also wanted to include the desk scene in the space, so I put it in as the starting point of the space. Since I tend to leave my workspace messy, I put most of the objects on the cutting mat. Some of the audio I used are sound effects that I thought were funny and they only play when you get close to them. Left the fog as light because I wanted some objects to be revealed as you get closer while also having too much fog. I also want to mention that every object, except for the desk space you start in, are models I found online.
